One night, hanging out, beneath a failing light,\
in the middle of night so bright,\
Shaking up, then waking up, them looking up, then dying up,\
it's all used up, every scrap.\
You think about it, you start to doubt it,\
that the truth will come to light.\
It's out of sight, black and white.\
The future after, mocking laughter, is fading deep into the dead of night, no light.

Standing alone, the shadows stretch and take the day.\
Trying and lying, pretending they never broke you down.

Well, who will face this madness? The heavy curtain must fall.\
You know the price of silence, it's pressing on us all.

The sirens fade, the noise dies down, and all that's left is dust,\
You don't need to ask the man in charge, he's only built on rust.\
No power holds you here, no order makes you stay,\
Sometimes the bravest choice is just to walk away — to find a brighter day.
