Laced my boots in the morning light\
The pitch was quiet, the air was tight\
They called the names, I stood in line\
Then walked away like it was fine

I traced her name in the window steam\
Heart held tight to a halfway dream\
I spoke too low, she looked away\
Said, "You'll be fine — just not today."

So what the silence means\
A busted string of dreams\
Orange sun fades\
It knows the place I used to hide
